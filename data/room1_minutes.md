## 会议纪要（草稿）  
### 要点  
- **人类能动性与自动化矛盾**：自动化浪潮既承诺解放人力，也导致失业，深圳工厂的视觉模型质检案例显示效率提升但人力角色被削弱。  
- **控制与责任归属**：需设计“有意义的人类控制”，而非形式上的“人类在环”；医疗领域AI诊断责任归属问题凸显问责机制缺失。  
- **可竞争性与系统设计**：系统需允许人类质疑与拒绝（contestability），当前设计缺乏“撤销按钮”；深圳机器人公司实践“远程操作回退”作为解决方案。  
- **教育与AI交互模式**：AI作为辅导工具可提升学习效果，但作为答案机器可能削弱批判性思维；教育场景需默认“苏格拉底模式”（提问优先）。  
- **劳动力转型与政策干预**：AI可能创造新岗位但分布分散，需社会安全网与终身学习基础设施；新加坡“技能未来”计划为参考案例。  
- **开源与治理**：开源模型分散能力，需在部署层面而非模型发布层面进行治理；国际标准需互认而非完全统一。  
- **审计与透明度**：真实审计需系统访问、基准测试与后果机制；需建立普通人可理解的AI评级系统（如医疗建议评分）。  
- **隐私与数据风险**：真实用户数据评估存在隐私风险，合成数据与联邦学习可缓解但引入新问题。  
- **事故报告机制**：借鉴航空业“无责报告”机制，建立行业级匿名事故数据库，推动企业透明化。  

### 结论  
- **人类能动性需通过系统设计保障**：而非单纯延缓AI发展，需确保人类在决策中的主导地位。  
- **默认设置即政策**：界面与默认值设计者承担政策制定责任，需纳入政策审查流程。  
- **审计需具备约束力**：需包含系统访问、基准测试与后果机制，推动透明与问责。  
- **跨领域协作与标准互认**：国际标准需兼容而非统一，借鉴药品监管的互认机制。

## Session Minutes (Draft)  
### Key Points  
- **Human Agency and Automation Tension**: Automation waves promise to free people but also displace them. Shenzhen factories using vision models for quality control show efficiency gains but reduced human roles.  
- **Control and Accountability**: Meaningful human control is needed, not just "human-in-the-loop" formalities. Medical AI diagnostics raise accountability questions.  
- **Contestability and System Design**: Systems must allow human challenge and rejection (contestability). Current designs lack "undo" buttons; Shenzhen robotics companies practice "teleoperation fallback" as a solution.  
- **Education and AI Interaction**: AI as a tutor improves learning, but as an answer machine may weaken critical thinking. Education scenarios should default to "Socratic mode" (questioning first).  
- **Labor Transition and Policy Intervention**: AI may create new jobs but in dispersed locations. Social safety nets and lifelong learning infrastructure are needed. Singapore’s SkillsFuture credits model is a reference.  
- **Open Source and Governance**: Open models decentralize capability, requiring governance at deployment rather than model release. International standards need interoperability, not uniformity.  
- **Auditing and Transparency**: Real audits require system access, benchmarks, and consequences. A user-friendly AI rating system (e.g., medical advice stars) is needed.  
- **Privacy and Data Risks**: Real-user data evaluations pose privacy risks; synthetic data and federated learning mitigate but introduce validity issues.  
- **Incident Reporting Mechanisms**: Borrow aviation’s "blameless reporting" to build an industry-wide anonymous incident database, encouraging transparency.  

### Conclusions  
- **Human Agency Requires System Design**: Not slowing AI, but ensuring human dominance in decision-making.  
- **Default Settings Are Policy**: Interface and default designers bear policy-making responsibility, requiring policy review.  
- **Audits Must Have Enforcement**: Include system access, benchmarks, and consequences to ensure transparency and accountability.  
- **Cross-Domain Collaboration and Standard Interoperability**: International standards need compatibility, not uniformity, mirroring pharmaceutical mutual recognition.
